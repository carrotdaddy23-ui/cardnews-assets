02 核心技术 Core Technology

为什么吃了能量胶
胃里会不舒服？

Why does an energy gel
upset your stomach?

普通能量胶是「高渗」产品。高浓度碳水一进胃就散开，会把周围的水分拉过来平衡浓度。而运动时流向胃肠的血液本来就减少 —— 胀气、烧心、不适就是这样来的。

A normal energy gel is a hypertonic product. Concentrated carbohydrate disperses as soon as it hits the stomach and pulls water in to balance concentration. During exercise, blood flow to the gut is already reduced — that is where the bloating and discomfort come from.

世界首创World First

pH 响应型水凝胶 · Hydro Energy Gel

pH-Responsive Hydrogel · Hydro Energy Gel

OLLLA 把碳水包裹进一层水凝胶结构里，利用人体不同部位的 pH 环境差异，控制碳水的释放时机 —— 在胃里保持凝胶不崩解，到小肠才打开。

OLLLA encapsulates the carbohydrate inside a gel structure and uses the pH difference between stomach and small intestine to control when it is released — the gel holds in the stomach and only opens further down.

HYPOTONIC 100

运动中补水，低渗透压比等渗更有利。HYPOTONIC 100 的渗透压为 149 mOsm/kg，远低于人体体液的 275~295 mOsm/kg —— 在胃里停留时间短、晃动感小，到小肠后靠「碳水 + 钠共同转运」把水一起带进体内。

For hydration during exercise, hypotonic beats isotonic. HYPOTONIC 100 measures 149 mOsm/kg against body fluid at 275–295 mOsm/kg — less time sitting in the stomach, less sloshing, and carbohydrate–sodium co-transport carrying water across in the small intestine.
